# 🛒 E-Commerce Analytics — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to analyze **e-commerce performance, customer purchasing behavior, conversion rates, and customer retention** across more than **2.5M view sessions**.

The project provides a business-focused view of the customer journey, helping identify **conversion bottlenecks, revenue opportunities, high-performing brands, and customer retention challenges**.

### 📊 Dashboard Highlights

* **2.5M+ View Sessions**
* **94K+ Purchase Sessions**
* **3.78% Overall Conversion Rate**
* **3.82M Total Revenue**
* **604K+ Cart Sessions**
* **884K+ Customers in Cohorts**

### 🎯 Key Questions Answered

* How effectively does website traffic convert into purchases?
* Where do customers drop off during the purchasing journey?
* Which brands generate the highest revenue and conversion rates?
* When are customers most likely to complete purchases?
* How does customer retention change over time?
* Where are the biggest opportunities to improve conversion and retention?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🛍️ Low Overall Conversion

Despite generating more than **2.5M view sessions**, the overall conversion rate is only **3.78%**, highlighting an opportunity to improve the customer purchasing journey.

### 🛒 Major Cart Drop-off

Only **15.6% of cart sessions** resulted in purchases, meaning approximately **84.4%** did not reach the final purchase stage.

### 🏆 Brand Performance

**Runail** generated the highest revenue at approximately **206.5K**, while **Severina** achieved the highest conversion rate at **41.9%**.

### ⏰ Peak Purchasing Hours

Purchasing activity is concentrated between **9 AM and 1 PM**, with **11 AM** recording the highest number of purchase sessions at approximately **6,600**.

### 🔄 Weak Early Customer Retention

Only **8.8% of customers returned during Week 1**, with retention declining to **4.1% by Week 4**.

* **Brand Performance Optimization** to prioritize high-performing brands.
* **Customer Retention Strategies** to improve early engagement.
* **Campaign Timing Optimization** based on purchasing patterns.
